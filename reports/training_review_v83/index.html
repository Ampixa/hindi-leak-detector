It trained on 2,689 variants from 385 source recordings and 184 lexemes; those variants are correlated, not 2,689 independent sources.

Cross-validation minimum removal recall increased from 81.3% for the matched phone/context arm to 90.4% with the pretrained acoustic branch, at the declared family-level operating rule. This is an observed task improvement, not established causal or Hindi-error accuracy.

Earlier experiments

One raw spectral candidate and one locally supervised vowel candidate were trained on k2’s M2 Ultra GPU. The supervised candidate used 2689 measured variants from 385 source recordings and 184 lexemes. Variants of one source are correlated examples. Development used 111 other sources. Twelve validation cells exclude both the held-out conditioning voice and phone-collision group; this does not establish morphological-root independence.

Both candidates missed the required short-vowel checks. Adding the locally supervised acoustic branch reduced cross-validation removal recall by 3.4% compared with its matched phone/context-only arm. The experiment failed its intended improvement test.

What remains

The next improvement must separate brief retained vowels from missing vowels across held-out voices. The pretrained branch is useful, but current calibration cannot meet both the short-vowel false-alarm and removal-recall requirements. A release still needs a frozen challenge on new words and voices plus additional independently localized real errors. Old test data, Swar feedback and the 208 fresh cross-system sentences remain outside fitting. No general accent, nationality or prosody accuracy is claimed.
