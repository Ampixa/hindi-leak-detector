We’re previewing Ampixa’s word-level pronunciation leakage detector for Nepali TTS. It flags suspected Hindi-like vowel omissions and vowel changes, points to the affected word, and lets you compare the audio with Nepali references.

It has caught हैन, किनकि, and an additional नेकपा case confirmed during testing. The research preview will include examples, controlled-test results and failure cases as we expand coverage.
