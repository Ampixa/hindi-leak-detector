Where the measurements fail

A 24-source training diagnostic found both misplaced gaps and weak short-vowel emissions. Claude then proposed a matched experiment using known edit geometry. We ran it on 379 training and 109 development source pairs, comparing retained short vowels, removals and original vocoded speech.

In the table, each partition’s diagnostic threshold permits about 1% flags on its retained-vowel controls. Known intervals did not rescue the blank-versus-vowel statistic.

Diagnostic partitionSourcesRemoval detection, inferred gapRemoval detection, known interval
Train37941.2%1.3%
Development10922.0%9.2%

Anchor-only redesign failed its declared gate. The known-interval statistic also has short-vowel and intervention-validity limits. This does not prove every source phoneme was correct or that every removal was phonetic deletion. Before another model fit, the next decision needs a better short-vowel representation and independently marked real-word intervals. The held-out test is spent.

HindiBench timing is feasible; its score needs more work

A persistent runtime processed 8 development utterances in a median 0.63 seconds, p95 0.77 seconds on k2. Median processing/audio duration was 0.089; cold initialization took 6.70 seconds.

This analyzes completed utterances with bidirectional encoders. It is not streaming. It tests supported internal-vowel deletion sites, reports word coverage, and provides no calibrated Hindi-influence probability or validated prosody score. The timed candidate failed admission.
